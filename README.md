Work through the prompts in order, feeding the output of each stage into the next, and adapt the inputs to the product you want to advertise.

---

## 🎬 The Workflow

**Product → Creator → Creator + Product → Animated UGC Ad**

1. Generate the product
2. Create the ideal creator
3. Combine the creator with the product
4. Animate the final UGC advertisement

---

## 🧠 What This Resource Does

This resource helps you produce UGC-style ads with AI — generating the product shot, building the creator who presents it, placing the two together in one scene, and animating the result into a finished advertisement with a voiceover.

---

## 📁 What's Inside

| Resource                                  | Description                            |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `prompts/product-image-prompt.txt`        | Generates the product image            |
| `prompts/creator-image-prompt.txt`        | Creates the ideal creator              |
| `prompts/creator-plus-product-prompt.txt` | Places the creator with the product    |

| `assets/`                                 | Supporting assets used in the workflow |

---

## ⚠️ Important

The creators generated in this workflow are not real people, and the footage is not a real customer review.

Follow the AI disclosure and advertising rules of whatever platform you publish on, and keep the claims you make about your product honest.
